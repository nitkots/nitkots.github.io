Privacy

Last updated November 2026

The short version

This is a personal portfolio. It has no accounts, no forms, no analytics and no advertising, and it doesn't set cookies or track you.

What the site stores

While you move between pages, your browser keeps a single flag in its session storage so the page transition can finish smoothly. It never leaves your device and is cleared when you close the tab.

The email address is only copied to your clipboard when you click a copy button, press C, or spell the word on the sphere.

Services that load parts of the site

Like most websites, some files are loaded from other services. When your browser requests them, those services receive standard request information such as your IP address and browser type, under their own policies:

  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the site.
  • Google Fonts, for the typefaces.
  • jsDelivr and cdnjs (Cloudflare), for the 3D sphere and PDF previews.

Links elsewhere

Links to GitHub, LinkedIn, X and other sites take you to services with their own privacy policies.

If you email me

I'll only use your message and address to reply to you, and I won't share them.
